# Air Cargo Simulator

A cargo delivery simulator add-on for [FlightGear](https://www.flightgear.org/).

## About
This add-on introduces a streamlined cargo delivery simulation to FlightGear, enhancing your flying experience with dynamic cargo transport tasks, career progression, and rewards.

## Features
- **Dynamic Local Deliveries**: Searches for the closest 25 airports within a 500 NM range and generates random cargo offers.
- **Progression System**: Complete deliveries to increase your budget and your pilot rank.
- **Statistics Tracking**: Monitor your delivery history and career progress.
- **Integrated Help System**: In-game guidance to get you started quickly.

## Requirements
- FlightGear 2024.1.0 or newer

## Installation
1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](https://github.com/RacerBG/air-cargo-simulator/releases) page (or clone the repository).
2. Enable Air Cargo Simulator in the FlightGear launcher under the "Add-ons" tab.

## Usage
Available cargo offers can be found at the airports.
The add-on was tested with the Cessna 172P, Cessna 182S and Piper PA28-161 Warrior II.
It is meant to be played with small single-engine aircraft.

In order to commence any cargo operations:
- You must be in the vicinity of an airport.
- The aircraft must be on the ground and completely stationary.
- The aircraft engine must not be running.
