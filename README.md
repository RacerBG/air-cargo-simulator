# Air Cargo Simulator – A cargo delivery add-on for FlightGear

## About
This add-on introduces a streamlined cargo delivery simulation to FlightGear, enhancing your flying experience with dynamic cargo offers, pilot progression, financial rewards, and penalties.

## Features
- **Dynamic Local Deliveries**: Searches for the closest 25 airports within a 500 NM range and generates random cargo offers.
- **Cargo Weight and Balance**: Automatically detects supported aircraft baggage compartments and adds cargo weight to the aircraft's weight and balance.
- **Landing Damage System**: Hard landings result in a failed delivery and a financial penalty.
- **Company Messages**: Receive messages from companies when accepting, completing, or failing a delivery.
- **Progression System**: Complete deliveries to earn money and increase your rank.
- **Statistics Tracking**: Monitor your budget, completed and failed deliveries, delivered cargo weight, covered distance, and rank.
- **Integrated Help System**: In-game guidance to get you started quickly.

## Requirements
- FlightGear 2024.1.0 or newer

## Installation
1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](https://github.com/RacerBG/air-cargo-simulator/releases) page (or clone the repository).
2. Enable Air Cargo Simulator in the FlightGear launcher under the "Add-ons" tab.

## Compatibility
This add-on is designed to work with a wide range of aircraft. Aircraft with supported baggage compartments are automatically detected, and cargo weight is added to the aircraft's weight and balance.

## Usage
Available cargo offers can be found at the airports.

In order to commence any cargo operations:
- **You must be in the vicinity of an airport.**
- **The aircraft must be on the ground and completely stationary.**
- **The aircraft engine must not be running.**
